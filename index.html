Jay-Z Fights to Revive Claims Against Attorney Tony Buzbee

Jay-Z, whose legal name is Shawn Carter, is asking a California appeals court to revive claims against attorney Tony Buzbee following litigation connected to a now-withdrawn sexual-assault lawsuit.

Carter argues that the earlier case was part of an effort to extort and defame him. Buzbee disputes Carter's allegations.

An appellate panel has indicated tentatively that it is inclined to leave the earlier dismissal in place. The Blind Truth is separating the original allegations, the withdrawal, Carter's counterclaims and what courts have actually established.

Tupac Murder Trial: Nearly 30 Years Later, What Does the Evidence Show?

Duane “Keffe D” Davis is on trial over the 1996 killing of Tupac Shakur. Prosecutors are relying heavily on Davis's previous statements, including a police interview, media appearances and his memoir.

The defense argues that Davis exaggerated or fabricated his involvement. The case is finally putting decades of hip-hop history, accusations and conspiracy theories inside a courtroom.

WHY THE BLIND TRUTH EXISTS

Evidence Before Assumption.

Don't believe something because everybody says it's true. Don't reject something because everybody says it's crazy. Investigate it.

The Blind Truth examines trending stories, entertainment, gossip, controversies, history and major claims without deciding the answer before looking at the evidence.

We look for original recordings, primary sources, credible reporting, court documents, historical records, contradictions, missing context and information that challenges the popular narrative.

And yes — we'll talk gossip. The difference is that rumors will be labeled as rumors, allegations as allegations and facts as facts.

The goal isn't to tell you what to think. The goal is to give you enough evidence to think for yourself.
