Our Mission

Investigate it.

The Blind Truth exists to examine claims instead of blindly accepting or dismissing them. We look at primary sources, official records, research, conflicting claims and available evidence before drawing conclusions. The objective isn't to tell readers what to believe — it's to give them enough information to examine the evidence themselves.
