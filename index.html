Why The Blind Truth Exists
Don't believe something because everyone says it's true. Don't reject something because everyone says it's crazy. Investigate it.

The Blind Truth exists to examine controversial ideas without deciding the answer before the investigation begins. We look for primary sources, credible reporting, historical records, scientific evidence, public documents, expert analysis, contradictions, and missing information.


Readers should be able to see what supports a claim, what challenges it, what remains unknown, and why a final verdict was reached.
