CASE FILE 004 • 9/11 • INVESTIGATION

9/11: Failure, Foreknowledge or Something More?

Twenty-five years later, The Blind Truth separates documented evidence from unsupported claims.

WHO PLANNED 9/11?

The strongest documentary record identifies Osama bin Laden, Khalid Sheikh Mohammed, Mohammed Atef and al-Qaeda as the principal organizers.

STRONGLY SUPPORTED Al-Qaeda planned and carried out the September 11 attacks.

WARNING SIGNS

U.S. agencies received repeated warnings in 2001 that al-Qaeda was preparing a major operation. The warnings did not identify the complete operational plan, date and targets.

INTELLIGENCE FAILURES

Future hijackers Khalid al-Mihdhar and Nawaf al-Hazmi were known to U.S. intelligence before 9/11, and critical information about them was not effectively shared.

DID THE GOVERNMENT LET IT HAPPEN?

The public record documents major failures of intelligence sharing, watchlisting and coordination. It does not establish an intentional order by U.S. officials to allow the attacks.

CLAIM: THE U.S. GOVERNMENT DELIBERATELY ALLOWED 9/11

STATUS: NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE PUBLIC EVIDENCE

THE SAUDI QUESTION

Saudi-linked assistance to future hijackers remains an area of scrutiny, but no public finding has established that Saudi Arabia as a government ordered 9/11.

WORLD TRADE CENTER

NIST concluded that aircraft impact, structural damage, loss of fireproofing and prolonged fires caused the Twin Towers to collapse, and that fire and progressive structural failure caused the collapse of World Trade Center 7.

IRAQ

The 9/11 Commission found no evidence that Saddam Hussein's government developed an operational partnership with al-Qaeda to conduct the attacks.
