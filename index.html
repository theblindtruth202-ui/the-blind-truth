SEPTEMBER 11 • 25 YEARS

America Marks 25 Years Since 9/11

September 11, 2026 marks a quarter-century since the attacks that transformed American life, foreign policy and national security.

Nearly 3,000 people were killed on September 11, 2001 when four hijacked aircraft were used in coordinated terrorist attacks against the United States.

Two aircraft struck the World Trade Center in New York. American Airlines Flight 77 struck the Pentagon. United Airlines Flight 93 crashed in Pennsylvania after passengers and crew fought back against the hijackers.

Commemorations on the 25th anniversary were held at Ground Zero, the Pentagon and the Flight 93 National Memorial.

The human cost did not end in 2001. Hundreds of firefighters and many other responders, survivors and workers have died in later years from illnesses linked to exposure at the disaster sites.

THE BLIND TRUTH

Twenty-five years later, remembering the victims and investigating the unanswered questions are not mutually exclusive. Both matter.
CASE FILE 004 • 9/11 • INVESTIGATION

9/11: Failure, Foreknowledge or Something More?

Twenty-five years later, we separate what is proven, what is unresolved, what was missed and what remains unsupported.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Did the United States government plan the September 11 attacks? Did officials know what was coming and deliberately allow it? Did intelligence agencies possess enough information to stop the plot? Did another government assist the hijackers? And was America later directed toward enemies that had little or nothing to do with the attack?

Those are separate questions. They require separate evidence.

EXHIBIT 1 — WHO PLANNED 9/11?

The strongest available documentary record identifies Osama bin Laden, Khalid Sheikh Mohammed, Mohammed Atef and the al-Qaeda network as the principal organizers of the aircraft plot.

Nineteen hijackers carried out the attacks. Mohammed Atta served as the operational leader of the hijacking team inside the United States.

FINDING: STRONGLY SUPPORTED Al-Qaeda planned and carried out the September 11 attacks.

EXHIBIT 2 — THE SYSTEM WAS BLINKING RED

During the spring and summer of 2001, American intelligence agencies received repeated reporting warning that al-Qaeda was preparing a major operation.

The later 9/11 Commission described the threat environment as one in which the system was effectively blinking red.

Those warnings did not identify the complete operational plan, date and targets.

But they clearly established that al-Qaeda posed an immediate and serious threat.

EXHIBIT 3 — BIN LADEN DETERMINED TO STRIKE IN U.S.

On August 6, 2001, President George W. Bush received a presidential intelligence briefing titled "Bin Ladin Determined to Strike in US."

The briefing discussed bin Laden's desire to attack inside the United States and referenced possible hijacking activity and an al-Qaeda support structure inside the country.

The document did not say that four aircraft would be hijacked on September 11.

But claims that the administration had no warning whatsoever are inaccurate.

EXHIBIT 4 — MIHDHAR AND HAZMI

Future hijackers Khalid al-Mihdhar and Nawaf al-Hazmi were known to American intelligence before 9/11.

Mihdhar possessed a valid U.S. visa. Both men eventually entered the United States.

Information concerning them was not effectively shared with FBI personnel at critical moments.

They lived openly inside America under their real names.

MAJOR DOCUMENTED FAILURE The 9/11 Commission concluded that locating and detaining Mihdhar and Hazmi might have disrupted the plot.

EXHIBIT 5 — THE PHOENIX MEMO

In July 2001, an FBI agent in Phoenix raised concerns about individuals connected to radical activity attending American aviation schools.

He recommended deeper investigation of flight schools and visa information.

The warning was not developed into a nationwide investigation before September 11.

EXHIBIT 6 — MOUSSAOUI

Zacarias Moussaoui was arrested in Minnesota in August 2001 after flight instructors became suspicious of his interest in training involving large commercial aircraft.

FBI personnel suspected a possible terrorism connection.

The information was never integrated with the Phoenix warning and the broader al-Qaeda threat picture in time.

EXHIBIT 7 — DID THE GOVERNMENT LET IT HAPPEN?

The public record demonstrates severe failures of intelligence sharing, bureaucratic coordination, watchlisting, analysis and urgency.

What it does not establish is an intentional order from U.S. officials to allow the attack to occur.

There is no publicly verified stand-down order, operational directive, payment trail or authenticated communication showing American officials knowingly facilitated the attack.

CLAIM: THE U.S. GOVERNMENT DELIBERATELY ALLOWED 9/11

VERDICT: UNPROVEN

Failure is documented. Intentional facilitation has not been established.

EXHIBIT 8 — THE SAUDI QUESTION

Fifteen of the nineteen hijackers were Saudi citizens.

Nationality alone does not establish government involvement.

However, future hijackers Mihdhar and Hazmi received substantial assistance after arriving in California from Saudi national Omar al-Bayoumi.

Bayoumi helped them find housing, co-signed a lease and assisted them in establishing themselves in San Diego.

EXHIBIT 9 — OMAR AL-BAYOUMI

Later declassified FBI material complicated the earlier public understanding of Bayoumi.

Investigators examined whether Bayoumi had connections to Saudi intelligence and whether assistance provided to the hijackers was knowingly connected to their mission.

That question remains disputed.

EXHIBIT 10 — OPERATION ENCORE

Years after the attacks, the FBI conducted a deeper investigation known as Operation Encore.

Investigators examined Bayoumi, Saudi diplomat Fahad al-Thumairy and Saudi embassy official Musaed al-Jarrah to determine whether a network knowingly assisted the hijackers.

No public finding has established that Saudi Arabia as a government ordered 9/11.

But the assistance provided to future hijackers remains one of the most important unresolved portions of the case.

EXHIBIT 11 — THE CAPITOL VIDEO

A video made by Bayoumi before the attacks showed him recording areas around the U.S. Capitol.

Later investigators have questioned whether the footage resembled pre-operational surveillance.

Bayoumi has maintained that it was innocent tourist footage.

The video alone does not prove participation in the 9/11 conspiracy.

EXHIBIT 12 — THE MONEY TRAIL

The September 11 operation is estimated to have cost roughly $400,000 to $500,000.

Much of the operational money has been traced through Khalid Sheikh Mohammed and the al-Qaeda network.

The hijackers used ordinary financial institutions and American bank accounts.

Investigators did not identify every original donor behind every dollar that ultimately reached al-Qaeda.

MONEY TRAIL: STRONG TO AL-QAEDA The operational financing is strongly linked to the al-Qaeda network. The ultimate source of every dollar was not established.

EXHIBIT 13 — INSIDER TRADING

Unusual trading activity, including put options connected to airline stocks, was investigated after the attacks.

Investigators traced the suspicious trades they examined and reported no established connection between profitable trading and terrorist foreknowledge.

CLAIM: INSIDERS PROFITED FROM SECRET KNOWLEDGE OF 9/11

VERDICT: NOT SUBSTANTIATED BY THE INVESTIGATION

EXHIBIT 14 — THE TWIN TOWERS

The National Institute of Standards and Technology concluded that aircraft impact, structural damage, loss of fireproofing and prolonged fires caused the collapses of the Twin Towers.

NIST reported no evidence supporting controlled demolition.

NIST did not, however, perform chemical testing of recovered Twin Tower steel for thermite or explosive residue.

That fact should be stated accurately.

The absence of that specific test is not itself proof that explosives were used.

EXHIBIT 15 — WORLD TRADE CENTER 7

World Trade Center 7 was not struck by an aircraft.

NIST concluded that fire and progressive structural failure caused the building's collapse.

Because the building was not hit by a plane, its collapse has remained central to many conspiracy theories.

No verified evidence has established that demolition charges were installed inside the building.

EXHIBIT 16 — IRAQ

The 9/11 Commission found no evidence that Saddam Hussein's government developed an operational partnership with al-Qaeda to conduct the September 11 attacks.

Iraq did not carry out 9/11.

The United States nevertheless invaded Iraq in 2003 during the broader post-9/11 War on Terror.

CLAIM: IRAQ HELPED CARRY OUT 9/11

VERDICT: NOT SUPPORTED

EXHIBIT 17 — AFGHANISTAN

Afghanistan was different.

Al-Qaeda operated from Afghan territory while receiving sanctuary from the Taliban.

That created a direct connection between Afghanistan and the organization responsible for 9/11.

Whether the entire twenty-year war was successful or justified at every stage is a separate question.

WHAT IS PROVEN?

PROVEN OR STRONGLY SUPPORTED Al-Qaeda organized and carried out the September 11 attacks.
PROVEN American intelligence agencies possessed significant warning information before the attacks.
PROVEN Major intelligence and information-sharing failures occurred.
STRONGLY SUPPORTED Missed opportunities involving future hijackers could potentially have disrupted the plot.
NOT PROVEN The United States government planned the attacks.
NOT PROVEN U.S. officials knowingly allowed the attacks to happen.
UNRESOLVED Whether Saudi-linked individuals knowingly provided operational assistance to the hijackers.
NOT SUPPORTED Iraq participated in the September 11 attacks.

THE BLIND TRUTH VERDICT

CASE FILE 004

PARTIALLY RESOLVED / CONTINUING INVESTIGATION

The evidence overwhelmingly supports the conclusion that 9/11 was an al-Qaeda operation.

The United States also experienced catastrophic intelligence, security and information-sharing failures before the attacks.

Those failures do not by themselves prove that American officials wanted the attacks to occur.

There is currently insufficient credible evidence to conclude that the U.S. government planned or deliberately permitted 9/11.

The Saudi-support question, particularly the assistance provided to Mihdhar and Hazmi and the later evidence surrounding Bayoumi, remains worthy of continued scrutiny.

HIP-HOP • FEDERAL COURT

Lil Durk Trial Now in Jury Hands

After testimony, cross-examination and closing arguments, jurors are now deciding whether prosecutors proved their case beyond a reasonable doubt.

Durk Devontay Banks, known professionally as Lil Durk, has pleaded not guilty to federal charges arising from an alleged murder-for-hire plot.

Prosecutors allege that the plot was retaliation connected to the killing of King Von and was aimed at rapper Quando Rondo.

Rondo survived. His cousin Saviay'a "Lul Pab" Robinson was killed.

THE PROSECUTION

The government called multiple cooperating witnesses who described the alleged planning, travel and execution of the attack.

Some witnesses directly implicated Durk.

THE DEFENSE

Durk's attorneys have argued that other individuals, including former assistant Kavon Grant, were responsible for directing the operation.

The defense attacked the credibility of cooperating witnesses, highlighting plea agreements, sentencing incentives, inconsistencies and earlier statements that conflicted with later testimony.

WHERE THE CASE STANDS

Closing arguments have concluded. The jury is deliberating.

Until a verdict is returned, Durk remains presumed innocent.

THE BLIND TRUTH STATUS: JURY DELIBERATING

Witness testimony is evidence. It is not automatically the truth. The jury must determine credibility and whether the government proved every required element beyond a reasonable doubt.

CASE FILE 003 • TUPAC • DIDDY

Did Diddy Have a Role in Tupac Shakur's Murder?

The million-dollar allegation, Keffe D's statements, gang politics, police evidence and decades of conspiracy.

THE QUESTION

Duane "Keffe D" Davis alleged that Sean "Diddy" Combs offered $1 million for Tupac Shakur and Suge Knight to be killed.

That allegation is documented.

It is not proven.

WHAT POLICE DID

Law enforcement attempted to corroborate parts of Davis's account.

Davis became a confidential informant and worked with investigators during efforts to verify his claims.

Those efforts did not publicly establish that Combs ordered Tupac's murder.

THE MONEY QUESTION

An alleged offer is not the same as an actual payment.

No publicly established financial trail has demonstrated that Combs transferred $1 million for Tupac's murder.

DOCUMENTED ALLEGATION Davis alleged that Combs offered money for Tupac and Suge Knight to be killed.
DOCUMENTED INVESTIGATION Police attempted to corroborate Davis's account.
NOT PROVEN Sean Combs ordered Tupac's murder.
NOT PROVEN Sean Combs paid $1 million for Tupac's murder.
NO TUPAC MURDER CHARGE Sean Combs has not been charged with Tupac Shakur's murder.

SEPTEMBER 11 STATUS

Keffe D's conviction does not materially change the evidence concerning Sean Combs.

The verdict established Davis's criminal responsibility.

It did not establish that Combs ordered, financed or participated in Tupac's murder.

CASE FILE 002 • NBA

Michael Jordan: A Real Choke Job?

Six rings. Zero Finals losses. But Finals record is not the same thing as an undefeated postseason career.

Michael Jordan is one of the greatest basketball players who ever lived.

Case File 002 examines whether the mythology surrounding his 6–0 Finals record sometimes erases postseason failures.

THE RECORD

Jordan won six NBA championships and six Finals MVP awards.

He also won five league MVP awards, ten scoring titles and one Defensive Player of the Year award.

Those accomplishments are real.

BUT 6–0 IS ONLY THE FINALS

Jordan played 37 playoff series and won 30.

That means his teams lost seven playoff series.

The famous 6–0 record describes only the NBA Finals.

1990 DETROIT

In Game 7 of the 1990 Eastern Conference Finals, Jordan scored 31 points with eight rebounds and nine assists.

Scottie Pippen scored two points on 1-of-10 shooting.

Horace Grant scored ten points on 3-of-17 shooting.

Calling that game a Jordan choke ignores the actual box score.

1995 ORLANDO

The 1995 Orlando series is more damaging to the invincibility myth.

In Game 1, Jordan scored 19 points, shot 8-for-22 and committed eight turnovers.

He also made critical late-game mistakes.

Chicago lost 94–91 and eventually lost the series four games to two.

THE BULLS WITHOUT JORDAN

In 1993–94, with Jordan retired, Chicago still finished 55–27.

The Bulls reached Game 7 of the Eastern Conference semifinals before losing to New York.

That does not prove Jordan was unnecessary.

It proves that Chicago was a legitimate high-level team around him.

THE RODMAN ADDITION

After Chicago's 1995 playoff loss, the Bulls added Dennis Rodman.

The following season, Chicago went 72–10 and won the championship.

Context matters.

CASE FILE 002 FINAL STATUS

MYTH OF INVINCIBILITY — DEBUNKED

Michael Jordan's GOAT argument is legitimate. His greatness does not require pretending he never lost, never struggled or never had an elite supporting cast.

6–0 in the Finals is historic. It is not an undefeated postseason career.
CASE FILE 001 • INVESTIGATION

Who Killed Charlie Kirk?

Evidence first. Rumors second. Verdict last.

Charlie Kirk was shot and killed while appearing at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah, on September 10, 2025.

Tyler James Robinson has been charged in connection with the killing.

He has pleaded not guilty.

PUBLICLY DESCRIBED EVIDENCE

The prosecution's case has included surveillance footage, a recovered firearm, DNA evidence, vehicle evidence, witness accounts and alleged digital communications.

Prosecutors have also described an alleged handwritten note and markings on ammunition.

THE FIREARM

A Mauser Model 98 .30-06 rifle was recovered wrapped in a towel in a wooded area.

Prosecutors have cited DNA evidence from multiple areas of the rifle.

Defense attorneys have emphasized that DNA evidence does not automatically prove when or how the DNA was deposited.

BALLISTICS

Testing of bullet evidence did not conclusively identify or exclude the recovered rifle as the source of the fatal projectile.

That does not eliminate the firearm from the case.

But it means the ballistic evidence should not be described as a perfect match if it was not one.

SEPTEMBER 2026 COURT UPDATE

A judge has now ruled that prosecutors presented enough evidence to require Robinson to stand trial.

That decision is a probable-cause ruling.

It means the judge found sufficient evidence for the prosecution to proceed.

It does not mean Robinson has been found guilty.

The prosecution is seeking the death penalty.

CASE FILE 001 — OPEN

The prosecution has cleared the probable-cause stage.

Robinson remains legally presumed innocent unless and until the government proves its case beyond a reasonable doubt at trial.

Alternative theories also require evidence. Unanswered questions are not themselves proof of a conspiracy.
