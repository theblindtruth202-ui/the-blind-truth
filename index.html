BREAKING • WORLD • MONEY

Oil Breaks $100 as Middle East Conflict Escalates

Energy markets are reacting to escalating conflict, shipping disruption and threats to major oil routes.

Brent crude moved above $100 per barrel on September 9, reaching its highest levels in weeks.

The move is being driven by escalating military conflict across the Middle East, attacks affecting energy infrastructure and growing concern about major shipping routes.

The Strait of Hormuz remains especially important. A large share of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas normally moves through the narrow waterway.

Shipping activity through Hormuz has remained below recent averages as conflict in the region continues to disrupt trade.

Higher crude prices can eventually affect gasoline, diesel, airline costs, shipping prices and inflation.

THE BLIND TRUTH STATUS: DEVELOPING

The rise above $100 per barrel is verified. The duration and economic consequences of the spike remain uncertain.
WORLD • ENERGY • SHIPPING

Strait of Hormuz Traffic Remains Disrupted

Commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz remains below recent traffic levels.

The waterway is one of the most important energy routes in the world.

Disruption matters because oil and liquefied natural gas from major Gulf producers normally travel through the strait.

Lower traffic, military attacks and uncertainty surrounding the region have contributed to higher energy prices.

STATUS: DEVELOPING

Shipping disruption is verified. The long-term effect on global supply depends on how long the conflict continues.

HIP-HOP • TUPAC • COURT

Tupac Murder: What the Verdict Solved — and What It Didn't

Keffe D's conviction creates legal accountability after nearly three decades. It does not answer every question surrounding Tupac's death.

Duane "Keffe D" Davis was convicted of first-degree murder in Tupac Shakur's 1996 killing.

The verdict establishes Davis's criminal responsibility for orchestrating and facilitating the retaliatory attack.

Prosecutors did not need to prove that Davis personally fired the fatal shots.

Davis's own interviews, recorded statements and memoir became important evidence against him.

WHAT WAS SOLVED

A jury determined beyond a reasonable doubt that Davis was criminally responsible for Tupac's murder.

WHAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED

The verdict does not judicially establish the exact shooter.

It does not establish that Sean Combs ordered the murder.

It does not establish that Combs paid for the murder.

It does not resolve every conspiracy theory that developed during the nearly 30 years before the conviction.

THE BLIND TRUTH FINDING

The legal question of Duane Davis's responsibility has been answered by the jury. The broader historical story still contains unanswered questions.
CASE FILE 003 • TUPAC • DIDDY

Did Diddy Have a Role in Tupac Shakur's Murder?

The million-dollar allegation, Keffe D's statements, gang politics, police evidence and 30 years of conspiracy.

THE QUESTION

Duane "Keffe D" Davis alleged that Sean "Diddy" Combs offered $1 million for Tupac Shakur and Suge Knight to be killed.

That allegation is documented.

It is not proven.

WHAT POLICE DID

Law enforcement attempted to corroborate parts of Davis's account.

Davis became a confidential informant and traveled with investigators to New York during an effort to reconnect with an associate he said linked him to Combs.

That operation did not publicly establish that Combs ordered Tupac's murder.

THE MONEY PROBLEM

An alleged offer is not the same as an actual payment.

Davis said he never received the alleged money.

No publicly established financial trail has demonstrated that Combs transferred $1 million for Tupac's murder.

DOCUMENTED ALLEGATION Davis alleged that Combs offered money for Tupac and Suge Knight to be killed.
DOCUMENTED INVESTIGATION Police attempted to corroborate Davis's account.
NOT PROVEN Sean Combs ordered Tupac's murder.
NOT PROVEN Sean Combs paid $1 million for Tupac's murder.
NO TUPAC MURDER CHARGE Sean Combs has not been charged with Tupac Shakur's murder.

SEPTEMBER 9 UPDATE

Keffe D's conviction does not materially change the evidence against Sean Combs.

The jury found Davis criminally responsible.

The verdict did not establish that Combs ordered, financed or participated in Tupac's murder.

CASE FILE 001 • INVESTIGATION

Who Killed Charlie Kirk?

Evidence first. Rumors second. Verdict last.

Charlie Kirk was shot and killed while appearing at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah, on September 10, 2025.

Tyler James Robinson has been accused in connection with the killing.

Publicly described evidence has included surveillance footage, a rooftop escape route, a recovered rifle, DNA evidence, vehicle evidence, witness statements, ammunition evidence and alleged digital communications.

An accusation is not a conviction.

Alternative theories require affirmative evidence. Unanswered questions alone do not prove an alternative theory.

CASE FILE 001 — OPEN

The prosecution theory appears substantially supported by publicly described evidence. However, the allegations must ultimately be judged through the legal process and admissible evidence.
