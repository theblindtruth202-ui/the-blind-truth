Why the receipts matter more than the loudest post.

CASE FILE 004 • 9/11

9/11: Failure, Foreknowledge or Something More?

The evidence, the failures and the claims that remain unsupported.

Who Organized the Attacks?

The 9/11 Commission identified al-Qaeda, led by Osama bin Laden, as responsible for planning and carrying out the attacks. Khalid Sheikh Mohammed was a central operational planner.

What Warnings Existed?

U.S. intelligence agencies received significant warnings about al-Qaeda threats before September 11, 2001. Those warnings did not provide the full attack plan, date and targets.

What Went Wrong?

Investigations documented serious failures in information sharing, watchlisting and coordination. Information about future hijackers Khalid al-Mihdhar and Nawaf al-Hazmi was not effectively shared.

Did U.S. Officials Deliberately Allow It?

The public evidence establishes major intelligence failures. It does not establish that U.S. officials ordered or intentionally permitted the attacks.

What About the Towers?

NIST attributed the Twin Towers' collapse to aircraft damage and subsequent fires, and World Trade Center 7's collapse to fire-induced progressive structural failure.

The Saudi and Iraq Questions

Saudi-linked assistance to future hijackers has been examined, but the public record does not establish that the Saudi government ordered 9/11. The 9/11 Commission found no operational partnership between Saddam Hussein's government and al-Qaeda to carry out the attacks.

CONCLUSION: Al-Qaeda responsibility and intelligence failures are well documented. Claims of deliberate U.S. government orchestration or permission remain unestablished by public evidence.

CASE FILE 002 • NBA

Michael Jordan — A Real Choke Job?

Six championships, six Finals MVP awards and a larger playoff story.

Michael Jordan won six NBA championships and six Finals MVP awards. His Chicago Bulls teams won every Finals series they reached during his playing career.

However, the Bulls were eliminated in earlier playoff rounds before their championship era, and Orlando defeated Chicago in the 1995 postseason following Jordan's return.

CONCLUSION: Jordan's 6–0 Finals record is accurate, but it does not describe every postseason series of his career. Calling him a failure based on selected losses or claiming he never lost in the playoffs both ignore context.

CASE FILE 001 • CHARLIE KIRK

Who Killed Charlie Kirk?

A criminal charge is not a conviction.

Charlie Kirk was fatally shot at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. Tyler Robinson was charged in connection with the killing and has pleaded not guilty.

Prosecutors have described evidence they believe links Robinson to the shooting. Defense attorneys dispute aspects of the prosecution's case.

A court's determination that sufficient evidence exists for trial does not establish guilt. The defendant is presumed innocent unless and until guilt is proven under the applicable legal standard.

LEGAL STATUS: Criminal proceedings are ongoing as described in the cited September reporting. Check the current court docket before publishing any new hearing or trial outcome.
