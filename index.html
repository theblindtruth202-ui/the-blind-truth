BREAKING NEWS • COURT • HIP-HOP

Keffe D Found Guilty in Tupac Shakur Murder

Thirty years after one of the most famous murders in music history, a Las Vegas jury has returned a guilty verdict.

Duane “Keffe D” Davis has been convicted of first-degree murder with a deadly weapon in connection with the 1996 killing of Tupac Shakur.

Shakur was shot in a drive-by attack in Las Vegas in September 1996 and died several days later. For decades, the killing became one of the most discussed unsolved cases in American music history.

THE PROSECUTION'S THEORY

Prosecutors did not have to prove that Davis personally fired the fatal bullets. Their case focused on the allegation that Davis helped organize and facilitate the retaliatory attack.

Davis's own public statements, interviews and writings became important pieces of the case. Prosecutors argued that those statements helped establish his role in the events leading to the shooting.

THE DEFENSE

The defense argued that Davis had exaggerated or fabricated stories about the killing for publicity, money and attention.

That created one of the central questions for jurors: Were Davis's own stories entertainment and exaggeration, or admissions describing his actual participation in the crime?

The jury ultimately accepted the prosecution's case and returned a guilty verdict.

THE BLIND TRUTH STATUS: GUILTY VERDICT RETURNED

The decades-long criminal case has now produced a jury verdict. A conviction is not necessarily the final step in the legal process. Sentencing, post-trial motions and an expected appeal can continue the case.
COURT • HIP-HOP • TRIAL UPDATE

Lil Durk Trial: Former Assistant Directly Implicates Rapper

The government's cooperating witnesses are now directly connecting Durk to the alleged murder-for-hire plot, while the defense attacks their credibility and motives.

Lil Durk, whose legal name is Durk Devontay Banks, is fighting federal allegations tied to an alleged murder-for-hire plot. He has pleaded not guilty.

Prosecutors allege the plot was connected to retaliation following the 2020 killing of King Von. The government has attempted to connect travel records, communications, vehicles, surveillance, financial activity and cooperating witnesses to its theory of the case.

KAVON GRANT TAKES THE STAND

Former Durk personal assistant Kavon Grant, also known as Vonnie, has become one of the government's most important witnesses.

Grant acknowledged his own participation in the August 2022 shooting that killed Saviay’a Robinson, the cousin of rapper Quando Rondo.

When prosecutors asked why the group carried out the attack, Grant testified that Durk had instructed them. That testimony represents one of the government's most direct attempts to connect Durk personally to the alleged plot.

WHY GRANT'S CREDIBILITY MATTERS

The defense has argued that Grant himself organized and planned the shooting. That makes his testimony unusually important.

If jurors believe Grant, his testimony could significantly strengthen the prosecution's case. If jurors believe he is shifting blame to reduce his own punishment, the same testimony could become a major weakness.

KEITH JONES

Keith “Flacka” Jones has also testified for prosecutors. His testimony included allegations about money connected to the attempted attack on Quando Rondo and later conversations involving Durk.

But Jones has admitted that he lied during an earlier interview with law enforcement. The defense has used those earlier statements and his cooperation agreement to challenge his reliability.

THE QUESTION FOR THE JURY

A cooperating witness does not become automatically truthful simply because he testifies for the government. He also does not become automatically untruthful because he received a plea agreement.

Jurors must determine whether the witnesses' stories are corroborated by independent evidence such as communications, travel records, surveillance, financial evidence and other testimony.

THE BLIND TRUTH STATUS: TRIAL ACTIVE — TOO EARLY TO CALL

The prosecution has now presented direct testimony connecting Durk to the alleged plot.

But the witnesses making those claims have their own criminal exposure and credibility issues that the defense is actively challenging.

Durk remains presumed innocent unless the government proves guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.
CASE FILE 001 • INVESTIGATION

Who Killed Charlie Kirk?

Evidence first. Rumors second. Verdict last.

SEPTEMBER 1, 2026 — COURT UPDATE Judge Tony Graf is considering whether prosecutors have established enough probable cause to order Tyler Robinson to stand trial. This stage does not determine whether Robinson is guilty. The question is whether the evidence is sufficient for the criminal case to proceed toward trial. The defense is also challenging the prosecution's case and arguments connected to the potential death penalty. Until the court rules and the case progresses further, Case File 001 remains OPEN.

Charlie Kirk was shot and killed while appearing at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah, on September 10, 2025.

Tyler James Robinson has been accused in connection with the killing. An accusation is not the same as a conviction, and the case must ultimately be judged on admissible evidence.

THE PHYSICAL EVIDENCE

Publicly described evidence has included surveillance footage, a rooftop escape route, a recovered rifle, DNA evidence, vehicle evidence, witness statements, ammunition evidence and alleged digital communications.

Each category of evidence must be examined independently before being combined into a larger theory. One weak piece does not automatically destroy an entire case, and one strong piece does not automatically prove every allegation.

THE RIFLE AND DNA

Investigators recovered a Mauser Model 98 .30-06 rifle wrapped in a towel in a wooded area.

Prosecutors have described DNA evidence linking Robinson to multiple parts of the recovered firearm.

DNA can establish that a person likely contacted an object. By itself, DNA does not automatically establish exactly when or why that contact occurred. That distinction matters.

BALLISTICS

Forensic reporting concerning projectile comparison has also raised questions. The publicly described comparison of a bullet fragment recovered from Kirk's body was inconclusive.

An inconclusive result does not mean the recovered rifle has been proven to be the murder weapon. But it also does not prove the rifle was uninvolved.

DIGITAL EVIDENCE

Prosecutors have described digital communications and writings they say connect Robinson to the killing.

The defense is entitled to challenge how those messages were obtained, whether they are authentic, whether they are admissible and what they actually prove.

SURVEILLANCE AND TIMELINE

Prosecutors say surveillance footage tracks a person connected to Robinson arriving in the area, changing clothing, reaching the roof of the Losee Center and fleeing after the shot.

Investigators have also described physical impressions and evidence on the rooftop.

CONSPIRACY CLAIMS

The investigation must also examine alternative theories that have circulated online, including claims of another shooter, planted evidence, fabricated messages, security involvement, government involvement, foreign intelligence involvement and an inside operation.

Those theories require affirmative evidence. A theory cannot become fact simply because unanswered questions remain.

THE PRELIMINARY HEARING

The current court proceeding is not the murder trial.

At this stage, the judge must determine whether the prosecution has presented enough evidence to establish probable cause and move the charges forward.

That is a substantially lower legal standard than proving guilt beyond a reasonable doubt at trial.

THE BLIND TRUTH PROVISIONAL VERDICT

Based only on the publicly described evidence, the prosecution theory appears substantially supported by multiple categories of evidence.

However, the allegations have not been proven at trial.

Questions remain concerning motive, forensic interpretation, witness credibility and the weight individual pieces of evidence should receive.

CASE FILE 001 — OPEN

The verdict will change if credible new evidence materially changes the record.
CASE FILE 002 • NBA • GOAT INVESTIGATION

Michael Jordan: A Real Choke Job?

Six rings. Zero Finals losses. Six Finals MVPs. But does Michael Jordan's mythology survive when we stop counting only the six seasons that ended with championships and investigate everything else?

Michael Jordan is one of the greatest basketball players who ever lived. That is not what this investigation is trying to disprove. The question is whether Jordan's greatness has been transformed into something much bigger: a mythology where Jordan never failed, never choked, always delivered when everything was on the line and therefore owns an unquestionable claim to being the greatest player in NBA history.

The Blind Truth is putting that claim under investigation. Not because Jordan wasn't great. Because greatness should survive the evidence.

EXHIBIT 1 — THE MOST POWERFUL NUMBERS IN THE JORDAN ARGUMENT: 6–0

Michael Jordan reached six NBA Finals with the Chicago Bulls. Chicago won all six. Jordan won Finals MVP all six times. That is one of the strongest accomplishments in NBA history.

6–0 Michael Jordan's record in NBA Finals series.

But 6–0 does not mean Michael Jordan was undefeated in the postseason. It does not mean Jordan never lost when a championship was at stake. It means that during the six seasons Chicago advanced all the way to the NBA Finals, Chicago finished the job.

Jordan experienced playoff elimination multiple times before reaching those Finals. Those losses simply don't appear inside the statistic “6–0.”

BLIND TRUTH FINDING: TRUE — BUT INCOMPLETE

Jordan was undefeated in NBA Finals series. That should not be rewritten as Jordan being undefeated in postseason competition.

EXHIBIT 2 — WHY DOES LOSING LATER COUNT AGAINST YOU MORE?

Imagine two superstar players. Player A reaches the conference finals and loses. Player B wins his conference, reaches the NBA Finals and loses there.

Player A's Finals record remains untouched. Player B receives a Finals loss.

Yet Player B actually advanced farther toward a championship.

That creates a major weakness in using Finals record by itself as a measurement of greatness. Losing before the Finals protects your Finals record. Reaching the Finals and losing hurts it.

Six-and-zero is a legitimate accomplishment. It is not a complete description of a player's postseason career.

EXHIBIT 3 — BEFORE THE DYNASTY, JORDAN LOST TOO

Jordan's championship career did not begin the moment he entered the NBA. Chicago suffered multiple postseason eliminations before becoming a dynasty.

1985 Milwaukee eliminated Chicago in the first round.
1986 Boston swept Chicago 3–0. Jordan was spectacular in the series, including his famous 63-point performance. This is not classified as a Jordan choke.
1987 Boston swept Chicago again.
1988 Detroit eliminated Chicago 4–1.
1989 Detroit eliminated Chicago again.
1990 Detroit eliminated Chicago for a third consecutive year.

None of those facts make Jordan a bad basketball player. Many of Jordan's individual performances during those losses were extraordinary.

They establish something simpler: Michael Jordan experienced postseason failure just like every other superstar.

EXHIBIT 4 — NOT EVERY LOSS WAS A JORDAN CHOKE

An evidence-first investigation cannot simply blame Jordan every time Chicago lost.

Chicago's 1990 Game 7 loss against Detroit provides a perfect example. Jordan produced:

31 POINTS 9 assists
8 rebounds

Scottie Pippen finished with only two points on 1-for-10 shooting.

Calling that particular game a Michael Jordan choke would ignore the evidence.

BLIND TRUTH RATING: NOT A JORDAN CHOKE

EXHIBIT 5 — ORLANDO 1995: NOW THIS ONE COUNTS

Game 1 of the 1995 Eastern Conference semifinals presents a much different story.

Chicago held a late lead. Nick Anderson stripped Michael Jordan. Orlando converted the turnover. Chicago then got another opportunity. Jordan committed another turnover.

19 POINTS 8-for-22 shooting
8 turnovers
Two damaging late-game turnovers

Chicago lost the game. Orlando eventually eliminated Chicago from the playoffs 4–2.

If another superstar scored 19 points, turned the ball over eight times and committed critical late-game mistakes during a playoff loss, the word “choke” would almost certainly enter the conversation.

BLIND TRUTH RATING: CONFIRMED FAILURE

Jordan's overall career does not make this individual performance disappear.

EXHIBIT 6 — JORDAN RETIRED. CHICAGO DID NOT COLLAPSE.

The 1992–93 Chicago Bulls finished the regular season 57–25. They won the NBA championship. Then Michael Jordan retired.

55–27 Chicago Bulls record without Michael Jordan during the 1993–94 season.

That was only two fewer victories than the previous championship season.

Chicago defeated Cleveland in the opening playoff round. They then pushed the New York Knicks to Game 7 of the Eastern Conference semifinals.

Those Knicks eventually reached the NBA Finals.

BLIND TRUTH FINDING: CHICAGO REMAINED A CONTENDER WITHOUT JORDAN

Jordan's absence hurt the Bulls, especially offensively. But his retirement did not transform Chicago into a lottery team.

EXHIBIT 7 — SCOTTIE PIPPEN WAS MORE THAN “JORDAN'S SIDEKICK”

When Jordan retired, Scottie Pippen became Chicago's primary superstar. The Bulls remained one of the NBA's best teams.

Chicago also added players such as Toni Kukoč and Steve Kerr, while Phil Jackson remained head coach and the triangle offense remained intact.

That context matters. It would be inaccurate to say the exact same roster simply removed Michael Jordan and won 55 games.

But it is completely accurate to say: Chicago lost Michael Jordan and remained a 55-win contender.

EXHIBIT 8 — THE NEXT SEASON SHOWS JORDAN'S VALUE TOO

The evidence does not all point against Jordan. And we aren't going to hide the evidence that helps him.

Chicago was less dominant during the following 1994–95 season. Jordan returned in March 1995 and Chicago immediately became more dangerous.

That is powerful evidence of Jordan's enormous value.

But another fact remains: Jordan returned. Chicago entered the playoffs. And Orlando eliminated them.

Jordan's Finals record stayed 6–0 because Chicago never reached the Finals that season.

That is exactly why Finals record should not be confused with total postseason record.

EXHIBIT 9 — THEN CHICAGO ADDED DENNIS RODMAN

The Bulls added Dennis Rodman before the 1995–96 season.

Chicago now featured: Michael Jordan. Scottie Pippen. Dennis Rodman. Toni Kukoč. Ron Harper. Steve Kerr. Luc Longley. And Phil Jackson.

72–10 Chicago Bulls 1995–96 regular-season record.

Jordan was the centerpiece of one of basketball's greatest teams. But it was still exactly that: a team.

EXHIBIT 10 — IF SIX RINGS MAKE JORDAN THE GOAT, WHAT DO WE DO WITH BILL RUSSELL?

PLAYER CHAMPIONSHIPS REGULAR-SEASON MVP
Bill Russell 11 5
Michael Jordan 6 5

Russell won five more NBA championships than Jordan while matching Jordan's five regular-season MVP awards.

The response is usually: Different era. Smaller league. Great teammates. Different competition.

Those arguments may be valid. But once context is introduced for Russell, context must also be introduced for Jordan.

EXHIBIT 11 — KAREEM CREATES ANOTHER PROBLEM

PLAYER CHAMPIONSHIPS MVP FINALS MVP
Michael Jordan 6 5 6
Kareem Abdul-Jabbar 6 6 2

Kareem won exactly the same number of championships as Jordan. Six.

But Kareem won six regular-season MVP awards. Jordan won five.

Jordan has the enormous advantage in Finals MVP awards. Six to two.

Again, the discussion must extend beyond “Jordan was 6–0.”

EXHIBIT 12 — ROBERT HORRY PROVES RINGS CANNOT BE EVERYTHING

ROBERT HORRY: 7 CHAMPIONSHIPS Michael Jordan: 6 championships.

Is Robert Horry greater than Michael Jordan? Of course not.

Because basketball fans already understand that championships are team accomplishments.

Individual greatness requires a much deeper evaluation.

EXHIBIT 13 — THE GOAT DOUBLE STANDARD

JORDAN LOSES BEFORE THE FINALS His 6–0 Finals record remains untouched.
ANOTHER SUPERSTAR REACHES THE FINALS AND LOSES The Finals loss becomes part of his permanent legacy.
JORDAN HAS A BAD PLAYOFF GAME Great players sometimes have bad nights.
ANOTHER SUPERSTAR HAS A BAD PLAYOFF GAME Choker.

The question is whether every GOAT candidate is being evaluated using the same measuring stick.

EXHIBIT 14 — SO WAS MICHAEL JORDAN ACTUALLY A CHOKER?

If “choker” means a superstar who routinely disappeared when pressure became greatest, the evidence does not support describing Michael Jordan's entire career that way.

Jordan's postseason résumé is far too dominant.

But if the question is whether Michael Jordan ever failed during critical moments, played badly in important games or contributed directly to major playoff losses:

YES. Michael Jordan was human too.

Those conclusions are not contradictory.

Michael Jordan was not a career choker.

Michael Jordan absolutely had individual failures, bad playoff performances and postseason losses.

THE SIX-AND-ZERO PROBLEM

Jordan's undefeated Finals record is one of the strongest pieces of evidence supporting his GOAT case.

But the record is sometimes used to create something it does not prove: basketball perfection.

Once we accept that, the GOAT debate has to examine the complete résumé.

Peak. Longevity. Scoring. Defense. Playmaking. Championships. MVP awards. Finals performance. Playoff failures. Supporting cast. Competition. Era. And individual impact.

THE BLIND TRUTH VERDICT

WAS MICHAEL JORDAN GREAT?
VERIFIED. One of the greatest basketball players who ever lived.

WAS MICHAEL JORDAN 6–0 IN NBA FINALS SERIES?
VERIFIED. Six appearances. Six championships.

DID MICHAEL JORDAN NEVER LOSE WHEN A CHAMPIONSHIP WAS AT STAKE?
FALSE. Jordan's teams suffered postseason eliminations before reaching the Finals.

DID MICHAEL JORDAN EVER HAVE BAD PLAYOFF PERFORMANCES AND CRITICAL FAILURES?
VERIFIED.

DID CHICAGO IMMEDIATELY COLLAPSE AFTER JORDAN RETIRED?
FALSE. The Bulls went 55–27 during their first full season without him.

DOES 6–0 MATHEMATICALLY PROVE JORDAN IS THE GOAT?
NO. GOAT status depends on how different categories of basketball greatness are weighted.

FINAL FINDING

Michael Jordan doesn't need mythology to be great.

The real Michael Jordan is impressive enough.

But the real Michael Jordan lost.

The real Michael Jordan got swept.

The real Michael Jordan had bad playoff games.

The real Michael Jordan made critical mistakes.

The real Michael Jordan needed teammates.

And the real Michael Jordan eventually climbed to the top of basketball six different times.

CASE FILE 002 FINAL STATUS: MYTH OF INVINCIBILITY — DEBUNKED

Michael Jordan's case for being the greatest basketball player of all time is legitimate.

His claim to basketball perfection is not.

Six-and-zero proves that every time Jordan reached the NBA Finals, his Bulls finished the job.

It does not erase everything that happened before those Finals.

If every player is going on trial for the GOAT crown, then every player should be judged under the same rules.

Including Michael Jordan.

Nepal Flood Death Toll Passes 1,000

One of the region's worst recent natural disasters continues to grow as rescuers reach previously isolated areas.

More than 1,000 people have now been confirmed dead in Nepal following catastrophic flooding and debris flows in the Himalayan region.

1,003 DEAD Reported in Nepal as of the September 1 update.
3,916 MISSING Authorities continue searching for thousands of people.

Rescue teams are using soldiers, heavy machinery, temporary bridges and search crews to reach communities cut off by destroyed roads and damaged infrastructure.

During a disaster of this scale, death and missing-person totals can change rapidly as new areas become accessible.
