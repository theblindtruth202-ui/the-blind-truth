WHY THE BLIND TRUTH EXISTS

Evidence Before Assumption.

Don't believe something because everybody says it's true. Don't reject something because everybody says it's crazy. Investigate it.

The Blind Truth examines breaking news, trending stories, entertainment, hip-hop, gaming, gossip, controversies, history and major claims without deciding the answer before looking at the evidence.

We look for original recordings, primary sources, credible reporting, court documents, historical records, contradictions, missing context and information that challenges the popular narrative.

And yes — we'll talk gossip. The difference is that rumors will be labeled as rumors, allegations as allegations and facts as facts.

The goal isn't to tell you what to think. The goal is to give you enough evidence to think for yourself.
