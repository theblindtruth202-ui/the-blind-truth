Biography

About Marion Goldring

Marion Goldring is an entrepreneur, writer, publisher and independent media creator. He founded The Blind Truth as a digital platform for examining news, public claims, controversies and unanswered questions through an evidence-focused approach.

Rather than limiting the publication to a single subject, Goldring developed The Blind Truth as a multi-category newsroom covering current events, investigations, entertainment, hip-hop, sports, gaming, government, technology, hidden history, unsolved mysteries and fact checking.

The publication's central philosophy is summarized by its motto: “Question Everything. Follow the Evidence.” The goal is not to assume that every controversial claim is true or false, but to examine what can actually be supported by available evidence.

Goldring's professional development has been shaped substantially by hands-on experience, independent learning, entrepreneurship, writing, digital publishing and the development of real-world projects.

Publishing Philosophy

How The Blind Truth Approaches a Story

01

Question the Claim

Start with the actual claim rather than assuming that popular opinion, a viral post or an official statement automatically provides the complete picture.

02

Examine the Evidence

Separate documented facts from allegations, rumors, interpretations and information that remains unknown.

03

Reach a Fair Conclusion

Present what the available evidence supports while clearly identifying uncertainty and unanswered questions.
