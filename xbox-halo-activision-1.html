Xbox Shake-Up: Activision Takes Over Halo as Microsoft Cuts 268 More Jobs

Microsoft is carrying out another major Xbox restructuring, shifting development of the next flagship Halo game to a purpose-built Activision team while eliminating 268 additional roles across its gaming organization.

By Marion Goldring • Founder & Publisher, The Blind Truth

Microsoft announced a major reorganization of Xbox studios that changes where several of the company's best-known franchises will be developed.

268 More Roles Eliminated

According to an internal memo reported by The Verge, Microsoft is eliminating 268 roles across Halo Studios, other first-party studios and Xbox Game Studios management and central functions. The company said the latest cuts are part of a broader restructuring announced earlier in 2026.

The Next Major Halo Moves to Activision

Activision will develop the next flagship Halo title through a new, purpose-built team that Microsoft says will be separate from the teams working on Call of Duty. A smaller Halo Studios group will continue supporting existing Halo games and the community.

The Restructuring Goes Beyond Halo

Rare and World's Edge are also moving under Activision's organization. Microsoft is combining Playground Games and Turn 10, while other studios are being realigned across the company's gaming groups.

Reporting also says Ninja Theory faces an uncertain future after Microsoft explored a sale without completing a deal.

WHAT IS CONFIRMEDMicrosoft is eliminating 268 additional Xbox roles. Activision is taking responsibility for development of the next major Halo title. Multiple Xbox studios and franchises are being reorganized.

What It Means

The reorganization changes the studio structure behind one of Xbox's signature franchises. What it means for the creative direction, release timing and long-term identity of Halo will depend on decisions and announcements that have not yet been made public.
